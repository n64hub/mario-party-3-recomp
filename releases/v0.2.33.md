In split-screen minigames, the screen's outermost lines and columns, which a TV hid on the real console, are drawn black: Picking Panic's leftover stripe and Awful Tower's strip at the right edge are gone. Like v0.2.32 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `6beb80ff822b96bcf85dcdb512e8b2b7969d8259`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.33-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.33-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- After Picking Panic, a red and yellow stripe stayed on the screen's bottom edge through the winners scene when the top team won. In Awful Tower, a thin strip of another picture showed at the right edge of the left player's side. Both were on the screen's outermost lines and columns, which a TV hid on the real console. These are now drawn black, like the sides of the 4:3 picture.
