The launcher's main menu has a picture behind it, and at a high frame rate the party setup's spinning star turns in one piece and the characters' feet swing smoothly as they run in the opening. Like v0.2.30 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `6beb80ff822b96bcf85dcdb512e8b2b7969d8259`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.31-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.31-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**New:**

- The launcher's main menu has a picture behind it, painted for the project by one of its players, with the title and the menu outlined so they read on it. It covers the window at any shape, cut at its sides in a narrower one.

**Fixed:**

- At a high frame rate, the party setup's spinning star came apart in the frames drawn between the game's own: its front and back were turned to different angles, so the star and its sign flickered as it spun. It now turns in one piece at the display's rate. The code the Mario Party recomps share moves onto its latest version for this.
- At a high frame rate, the characters' feet stepped as they ran to the Millennium Star in the opening: in one frame of each stride a foot jumped ahead in the frame drawn between the game's own, then stood still. The feet now swing through smoothly.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a physical pad (it was played in the browser with stand-in pads), and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-3).
