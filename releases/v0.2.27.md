Hand Line and Sinker's fishing line is drawn, and the crowds lose the green outline round them. Like v0.2.26 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `6beb80ff822b96bcf85dcdb512e8b2b7969d8259`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.27-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.27-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed** ([#104](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp/issues/104)):

- In Hand Line and Sinker, the white fishing line runs from each rod into the pond again. The game draws it with the N64's line drawing, which the renderer used to leave out.
- The crowds' Toads, Goombas, Koopas and Baby Bowsers (Slot Synch's stands among them) no longer have a thin green outline. The green is the colour their sprites keep for the see-through part round them, and smoothing the sprites at a higher resolution used to blend it into their edges.

**Gamepads:** 32 more pads are recognised with their buttons in place, from SDL's controller database.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a physical pad (it was played in the browser with stand-in pads), and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-3).
