At a high frame rate, the title screen's logo is drawn whole at each size as it bounces after landing, no longer big and small at once in the frames drawn between the game's own. Like v0.2.31 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `6beb80ff822b96bcf85dcdb512e8b2b7969d8259`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.32-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.32-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- At a high frame rate, the title's logo was drawn big and small at once in some frames between the game's own as it bounced after landing: some of its strips were drawn at the size of the frame before and the rest at the new one. It is now drawn whole at each size. The code the Mario Party recomps share moves onto its latest version for this.
