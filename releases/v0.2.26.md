The opening's flash, the rules screen's book and Ice Rink Risk's rink now draw as on the console. Like v0.2.25 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `6beb80ff822b96bcf85dcdb512e8b2b7969d8259`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.26-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.26-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed** ([#96](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp/issues/96)):

- The Millennium Star's flash of white in the opening covers the whole screen. The opening draws it inside its camera's frame, 8 pixels in from the screen's edges, which a TV's overscan hid; it stopped short of the picture's top and bottom.
- The book on a minigame's rules screen no longer shows thin light lines down its cover. The cover is eight strips with half a unit of space between each pair, a third of a pixel at the console's resolution, which its antialiasing covered; at a higher resolution the gaps opened. Each strip now reaches under its neighbour.
- In Ice Rink Risk, a character who falls off the rink goes behind it again instead of showing through it. The rink hides what is below it with an invisible shape that writes only depth, and the renderer skipped it; it is now drawn in a mode the renderer keeps, which looks the same on the console.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a physical pad (it was played in the browser with stand-in pads), and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-3).
