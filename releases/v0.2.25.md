Duel boards fill a wide window and their backgrounds are smooth at a high resolution. Like v0.2.24 it contains no game data: you need your own ROM of the USA release (a big-endian `.z64` of 32 MiB, SHA-1 `6beb80ff822b96bcf85dcdb512e8b2b7969d8259`). Other regions are not supported.

> **Made with AI.** Almost all of the code was written by AI agents (Claude), directed and play-tested by a person.

**Windows:** unzip `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.25-windows-x64.zip` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled.exe` from that folder (it needs a recent Visual C++ 2015-2022 redistributable, x64). **Linux (x86-64):** extract `MarioParty3Recompiled-v0.2.25-linux-x64.tar.gz` and run `MarioParty3Recompiled` in that folder (glibc 2.35 or newer, a Vulkan driver, GTK 3 and FreeType; it brings its own SDL2). Pick your ROM with **Load ROM**, then **Start Game**: the ROM is stored, so the next start goes straight to the game.

**Fixed:**

- A duel board's background no longer shows straight lines across it at a resolution higher than the console's ([#93](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp/issues/93)). Each 64x48 piece of the background was loaded in two parts, each without the pixels next to it, so the smoothing stopped short at every part's edge. Duel boards now draw their background the way the other boards do, each piece loaded with its neighbours' edge pixels.
- Duel boards now fill a window wider than 4:3, as the other boards do, rather than drawing their background in a 4:3 box with black beside it. The board's camera stops where the wider picture reaches the map's edge.

**Not checked yet:** long play sessions, rumble on a physical pad (it was played in the browser with stand-in pads), and the Linux build on a GPU (it has been booted on Mesa's software Vulkan only). Reports are welcome on the [issues page](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp/issues).

The [README](https://github.com/n64hub/mario-party-3-recomp#readme) has what works and how to build it yourself. The same game also plays in a browser, with your ROM, at [n64hub.com](https://n64hub.com/#mario-party-3).
